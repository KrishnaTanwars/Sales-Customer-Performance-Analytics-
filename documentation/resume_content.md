## 1. Resume Bullet Points (Select 3–4 for your resume)

- **Engineered an end-to-end MySQL 8+ analytics database** over **105,000+ sales transaction records** ($167.69M revenue), structuring normalized relational schemas, foreign key constraints, composite indexes, and automated data validation scripts.
- **Architected 30+ complex SQL queries and 5 production analytical views** using multi-step CTEs, window functions (`RANK`, `DENSE_RANK`, `LAG`, `LEAD`), and subqueries to evaluate YoY sales velocity, customer lifetime value, and product category profit margins (39.74% global GM).
- **Extracted key commercial insights** identifying a -$40.80M target-to-actual revenue gap across 10 global markets; developed data-driven recommendations to restructure underperforming regional quotas and increase high-margin accessory attach sales by +4.0 percentage points.
- **Performed Pareto (80/20) revenue concentration analysis** demonstrating that 68% of enterprise revenue originates from top corporate resellers; established customer Segmentation models (Platinum to Bronze tiers) to prioritize retention efforts.

- **Advanced SQL Techniques:** Common Table Expressions (CTEs), Window Functions (`RANK()`, `DENSE_RANK()`, `ROW_NUMBER()`, `LAG()`, `LEAD()`, `SUM() OVER()`, `AVG() OVER()`), Correlated Subqueries, Analytical Views, Indexing & Query Optimization.
- **Business Analysis & Metrics:** Revenue Modeling, Gross Profit & Margin %, Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth, Year-over-Year (YoY) Performance, Target Variance & Achievement %, Average Order Value (AOV), Customer Segmentation, Pareto 80/20 Analysis.
- **Data Engineering & Quality:** Database Normalization (3NF), Schema Design, Foreign Key Constraints, `LOAD DATA INFILE`, Data Quality Auditing, Duplicate & Orphan FK Detection.
- **Documentation & Presentation:** Technical Writing, Data Dictionary Architecture, Entity-Relationship Diagramming (Mermaid), Executive Storytelling, Video Presentation Scripting.
