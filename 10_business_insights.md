## Executive Summary of Insights

1. **Total Revenue & Margins:** Generated **$167,693,927.24** in total revenue across **105,000 transactions**, achieving **$66,641,864.24** in gross profit (39.74% gross margin).
2. **Top Accounts:** Top accounts like Vanguard Digital #63 ($2.25M) and Beacon Enterprise #150 ($2.25M) lead spend, with 68% of sales coming from Corporate Resellers & Distributors.
3. **Category Profitability:** Laptops & Desktops lead top-line volume ($57.43M revenue, 29.22% GM), while Peripherals (49.31% GM) and Storage ($40.11M revenue, 44.75% GM) generate peak margins.
4. **Target Shortfall:** Global sales target was $208.50M vs $167.69M actuals (-$40.80M variance; 80.43% overall achievement).
5. **Regional Extremes:** North America West achieved 92.40% target achievement, while LATAM Brazil (35.02%) and Middle East Dubai (35.05%) underperformed significantly.
